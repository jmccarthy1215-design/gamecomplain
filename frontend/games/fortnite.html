🆕 Game Facts

  • Developed by Epic Games; launched in 2017 as a PvE game before the Battle Royale mode exploded in popularity
  • Free-to-play on PC, consoles, and mobile — over 350 million registered accounts
  • Seasonal content model with a new Chapter/Season every few months
  • Building mechanics are a core differentiator — controversial with "no-build" players who prefer Zero Build mode
  • Regular crossover events with Marvel, Star Wars, NFL, and more
  • FNCS (Fortnite Champion Series) competitive circuit with multi-million dollar prize pools
