🆕 Game Facts

  • Released December 2015 by Ubisoft Montreal; still actively updated in 2025
  • Peak concurrent players regularly exceed 100,000 on Steam with millions of registered players
  • Tactical 5v5 with destructible environments — a core differentiator in the FPS genre
  • Over 60 operators across Attack and Defense, each with unique gadgets and abilities
  • Siege Pro League runs multi-season globally with teams sponsored by major esports orgs
  • Reputation system introduced to combat toxic behavior in ranked modes
