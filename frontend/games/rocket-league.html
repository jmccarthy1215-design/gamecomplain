🆕 Game Facts

  • Released July 2015 by Psyonix; acquired by Epic Games in 2019
  • Went free-to-play in September 2021, massively boosting player counts
  • Consistently ranks among the top 10 most-played games on Steam with millions of active players
  • Competitive scene: Rocket League Championship Series (RLCS) with multi-million dollar prize pools
  • Cross-platform play available across PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and Switch
  • Seasonal ranked system: Bronze through Supersonic Legend
