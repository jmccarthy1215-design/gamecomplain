🆕 Game Facts

  • Developed and published by Supercell; released globally in March 2016
  • Over 100 million downloads — one of the highest-grossing mobile games ever
  • Real-time 1v1 and 2v2 battles using a deck of 8 cards with elixir as a resource
  • Regular balance updates change the meta — a constant source of community debate
  • Path of Legends replaced the old league ladder system in 2022
  • King's Cup and other official tournaments run with real prize pools
