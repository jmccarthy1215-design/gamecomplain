🆕 Game Facts

  • Developed by Riot Games; launched June 2020 as a free-to-play tactical FPS
  • 25+ agents each with unique abilities — ability combinations drive much of the meta debate
  • Vanguard anti-cheat runs at kernel level — one of the most discussed topics in the community
  • Competitive ranked system: Iron through Radiant, with strict MMR-based placement
  • VCT (Valorant Champions Tour) is the premier esports circuit with teams backed by Riot partnerships
  • Regular act updates every ~2 months bring new agents, maps, and balance patches
